## About

I am currently pursuing the Ph.D. degree at  
[Tongji University](https://en.tongji.edu.cn/) and [Zhangjiang National Laboratory](https://www.zjlab.ac.cn/index).

My research interests include **computational imaging**, **hyperspectral image fusion**, **infrared radiometric calibration**, and **AI-based temperature control**.
